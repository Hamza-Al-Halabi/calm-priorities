CALM PRIORITIES — WINDOWS
=========================

RUN IT
1. Keep CalmPriorities.exe and config.json in the same folder.
2. Double-click CalmPriorities.exe. Your browser opens the app.
   (First time, SmartScreen may warn: "More info" -> "Run anyway".)

EVERY DAY
- Today:  your plan for the day + suggestions (overdue, carried over, urgent).
- Week:   bars per day, weekly goal, wins, what's still open. Use the arrows
          to look at earlier weeks.
- Board:  the three priority boxes. Drag a card to change its priority.

YOUR DATA
- Saved in state.json next to the exe (backup: state.backup.json).
  Clearing your browser no longer loses anything. Back up that folder
  if you want extra safety.

MORNING EMAIL AT 7:00 (one-time setup, ~3 minutes)
Sending needs a mail account. Easiest: Gmail with an "App password".
1. Google Account -> Security -> turn on 2-Step Verification.
2. Search "App passwords" in your Google Account, create one (name it
   "Calm Priorities"), copy the 16 letters.
3. Open config.json and fill in:
      "smtpUser": "hamzaalhalabi99@gmail.com",
      "smtpPass": "the 16 letters"
   (defaultEmail is already hamzaalhalabi99@gmail.com)
4. Restart the exe. In the app: Settings -> "Send a test now".
Time and on/off live in Settings -> Morning briefing.

IMPORTANT: the exe must be running at 7:00. If the PC is off, the email
goes out as soon as the app starts (up to 8 hours after the set time).
To start it automatically when you log in, double-click
Start-With-Windows.bat once. For a 7:00 email even with the PC off, the
reminder server must run in the cloud (see README, section 5).

GOOD TO KNOW
- Keep the window open (minimized is fine).
- Port 5173 busy? Change "port" in config.json and restart.
- config.json holds your password: don't share that file.
- Resend (resendApiKey) still works as an alternative, but its test
  sender only delivers to the address you signed up to Resend with.
